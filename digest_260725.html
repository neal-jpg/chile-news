Top Headlines
Economy · Congress

Kast's Mega Economic Reform Is One Vote From Becoming Law

Chile's Chamber of Deputies passed the core of President José Antonio Kast's sweeping economic overhaul on Tuesday July 22 by an 85–59 vote, handing him the biggest legislative victory of his six-month-old government. The bill — formally the Law of Reconstruction and Economic and Social Development — cuts the corporate income tax rate from 27% to 23% by 2029, exempts newly built homes from VAT for one year, locks in a 20-year tax-stability regime for investments over $350M USD, and funnels reconstruction money to wildfire-damaged regions. Only a single disputed article remains: how to compensate municipalities that will lose property-tax revenue under the reform. A joint Chamber-Senate committee is expected to resolve it in the coming days, after which Kast will sign it into law.

The opposition immediately threatened a Constitutional Court challenge — Chile's version of the US Supreme Court — arguing several provisions favor corporations over citizens and undermine social spending. Left-wing lawmakers also objected to the near-party-line vote, noting the Senate approved key tax articles by a razor-thin 26–24 margin. Finance Minister Jorge Quiroz called the vote "the beginning of Chile's economic takeoff." Opponents called it a gift to the wealthy in the middle of budget cuts to elderly programs.

Trade · United States

Trump's 12.5% Tariff on Chile Now in Force — Salmon, Wine, and Fruit Feel the Pinch

The 12.5% US tariff on Chilean goods — announced Wednesday and effective Thursday July 24 — is now a daily reality for exporters. Washington's rationale under Section 301: Chile and 59 other countries have "failed to prohibit" the import of goods made with forced labor. Notably, copper — Chile's $8B-a-year export to the US — is excluded under a separate national-security exemption. What's not excluded: salmon, fresh fruit (grapes, blueberries), wine, and wood products. The farming lobby group SNA (roughly the Chilean Farm Bureau) put the annual damage to the agriculture sector at hundreds of millions of dollars.

The Kast government's position is awkward: it has spent months cultivating a close relationship with the Trump administration, signed strategic tech agreements, and positioned Chile as Washington's most reliable South American partner. That friendliness didn't buy an exemption. The trade undersecretary said Chile will negotiate to remove key products from the tariff list, while making clear Santiago won't retaliate. Brazil and Japan — also hit — said they will push back harder.

Infrastructure · Regional

Main Chile–Argentina Mountain Pass Closed for Days After Storm Piles Snow

Paso Los Libertadores — the main road link between Santiago and Mendoza, and the busiest border crossing in South America by cargo volume — has been shut for more than 72 hours after a major frontal storm dumped heavy snow on the Andes. The Mendoza provincial government said clearing the route "will require about five or six more days," meaning the crossing could remain closed into early next week. A binational emergency protocol kicks in automatically when the closure exceeds 72 hours.

The Paso handles a huge share of Argentina-Chile trade — it's the Route 66 of South American cross-border commerce. Truck queues stretch back dozens of kilometers on the Argentine side. Chilean officials activated logistics detours, but there is no realistic alternative of similar capacity. Travelers planning Mendoza weekend trips should reschedule.

Public Safety · Policy

Chile Eyes Overhaul of Police Incentives to Stem Carabineros Brain Drain

A legislative proposal advancing through Congress would modernize the pay, career incentives, and working conditions for Chile's national police force (Carabineros) and incorporate civilian staff for administrative work — freeing uniformed officers for street duties. The reform also includes new technology investments. The driver: Chile has seen a retention problem in Carabineros as experienced officers leave for better-paying private security jobs, even as the government has made crime and public safety its signature issue.

Think of it like a proposal in the US to give police departments competitive salaries and better gear to prevent departures to private contractors — politically popular with the right, and hard for the left to oppose outright even if the details are contested.

"Trump's tariffs are a powerful weapon — but they can't build a new world order"

An analysis piece from Radio U. Chile's news desk argues that Trump's forced-labor tariff sweep — hitting 60 countries including Chile, Japan, and Brazil — reveals a fundamental incoherence: the White House uses trade as punishment but has no coherent multilateral vision to replace what it dismantles. Chile, the piece notes, bent over backward to be a "model partner" and still got penalized. The lesson for Santiago: stop seeking bilateral favor and double down on trade diversification, particularly toward Asia and the EU.

"A week of victories and vulnerabilities for Chile's economic model"

DF's economic desk offers a measured take on Chile's turbulent week: the megarreforma passage was a genuine structural achievement — corporate taxes nudging closer to OECD norms, investment stability mechanisms that could revive foreign capital inflows. But the Trump tariff, arriving the same week, exposed how much Chile's export sectors still depend on a single large buyer. The editorial's implicit argument: the megarreforma matters precisely because Chile needs to be competitive enough to pivot when Washington turns unfriendly. A right-leaning pro-market read, notable for acknowledging that Kast's friendly posture toward Trump did not protect Chilean producers.

Post-Rock Prog

Hesse Kassel — Morir Saltando

The Santiago quintet's second album is drawing serious attention in the Chilean indie press and internationally on Rate Your Music (82/100, 700+ ratings). The opening track "Pornomiseria" runs nearly 15 minutes; the rest of the album builds on that sprawling ambition with tight, virtuosic playing and bleak social commentary. One of the strongest records to emerge from la nueva escena chilena this year. Available on Bandcamp and streaming.

For fans of: Slint, Godspeed You! Black Emperor, Trespass Trio.
