Trade & Diplomacy

Kast's Trump Gamble Hits a Wall: Chile Faces 12.5% U.S. Tariff Despite Playing Ally

The U.S. Trade Representative slapped Chile with a 12.5% tariff on roughly 40% of its exports to the United States, citing inadequate protections against goods made with forced labor — a blow that lands especially hard because President Kast's entire foreign policy has been built around ideological closeness to the Trump administration. Analysts published today say the alignment strategy "did not bring Chile the benefits the government expected." Defense Minister Fernando Barros went further, publicly questioning the "reliability" of the Chile-U.S. relationship.

The tariff hits salmon, avocados, kiwis, citrus, wine, and timber hardest; copper, lithium, and other raw materials (about 60% of exports) remain exempt. Chile's Foreign Ministry and business associations are now coordinating a joint negotiating strategy ahead of a visit by Jeffrey Goettman — the #2 official at the USTR — scheduled for later in August. It's the first USTR high-level visit to South America under this administration, and Chile is pressing to add its top agricultural exports to the exempt list.

The political sting is real: Kast modeled his approach on the idea that shared ideology with Trump would translate to preferential treatment — similar to how close EU allies expected carve-outs during the first Trump-era steel tariffs, only to find out trade policy is transactional, not relational. Opposition parties (including the center-left DC) are using the episode to argue that ideological affinity is no substitute for institutional trade diplomacy.

Security

Kast Creates 7 Specialized Crime Units — Minister Admits Chile "Looked From Afar" at Organized Crime

Interior Minister Martín Arrau — who replaced the fired Trinity Steinert after just 69 days in office this spring — unveiled seven new specialized task forces to tackle organized crime: border control, kidnapping, contract killings (sicariato), cybercrime, illicit markets, criminal financing, and violence in Chile's southern Araucanía region.

Speaking at an international organized-crime forum, Arrau acknowledged the structural problem plainly: "For a long time Chile believed it could look at this threat from afar." Critics, including the IPS news agency, argue Kast's approach leans too heavily on punitive measures and risks criminalizing youth rather than addressing root causes — a debate familiar to U.S. readers from the "tough on crime" vs. prevention arguments of the 1990s-2000s.

Economy & Social Policy

Pension Overhaul Advances: August Kicks Off New Disability-Insurance Structure as Employer Contributions Keep Rising

August marks a notable implementation date in Chile's ongoing pension reform. Starting this month, the Seguro de Invalidez y Sobrevivencia (SIS) — the mandatory disability and survivor insurance, think Social Security's disability arm — shifts from AFPs (Chile's private pension administrators, roughly like 401(k) managers) to a new Autonomous Protection Fund (FAPP). The move is designed to delink disability coverage from market-tied managers.

Separately, employers are now paying a 2% additional contribution on top of the standard 10% worker-only rate, part of a gradual 7-percentage-point increase to be phased in over nine years. The Pensión Garantizada Universal (PGU) — Chile's guaranteed floor pension, like a Social Security minimum — is set to increase to $250,000 Chilean pesos monthly for retirees over 75 starting in September. Chile's pension debate has been simmering for a decade; the Kast government inherited the Boric-era reform law and is implementing it while resisting any further expansion.

Science

The World's Most Powerful Cosmic Ray Observatory Is Being Built in Chile's Atacama

The South Wide-field Gamma-ray Observatory (SWGO) is breaking ground at Chile's Atacama Astronomical Park — a $60 million international collaboration that will detect gamma rays from some of the most violent events in the universe: supernovae, black hole jets, and dark matter annihilation. Chile already hosts ALMA (the world's most powerful radio telescope array), the Very Large Telescope (VLT), and the future Extremely Large Telescope (ELT). The SWGO adds an entirely different sensing modality: instead of catching light, it catches the secondary particle showers that high-energy gamma rays create when they hit Earth's atmosphere.

For context: the best U.S. gamma-ray observatory equivalent is HAWC in Mexico, a joint U.S.-Mexico project. Chile becoming the gamma-ray capital of the southern hemisphere is a genuine scientific milestone — and one that benefits from the Atacama's altitude (over 4,400 meters), clear skies, and low population density.

Opinion · Left

"Residuos de catástrofe: el costo de no planificar lo previsible"

El Mostrador · August 1, 2026

A column in the left-of-center El Mostrador argues that Chile's municipalities are systematically unprepared for post-disaster waste management — most communal waste plans are outdated or non-existent. The January 2026 wildfires that devastated Biobío and Ñuble regions exposed this gap acutely: months later, debris clearing and toxic-waste sorting remain chaotic. The author's argument maps onto a familiar American failure mode — "disaster capitalism" preparedness that plans for emergency response but not for the unglamorous, years-long cleanup.

Darkwave Indie

Diavol Strâin — Eterno Retorno

The Valparaíso/Santiago duo's fifth album (released March 29 via Bat-Cave Productions) has been quietly becoming the most internationally acclaimed Chilean record of 2026. The U.S. electronic/dark-music press I Die: You Die called it "potentially the best darkwave record of 2026 — a bracing and full-bodied blast of maximalist darkwave." Nine tracks of dense, Spanish-language gothic post-punk produced with William Faith at 13 Studio (a veteran of Christian Death, Faith and the Muse). For fans of: Clan of Xymox, Lebanon Hanover, late-period Bauhaus.

The album is available on Bandcamp and Spotify; a limited CD digipack (150 copies) sold out quickly through Bat-Cave Productions in Poland. A genuinely Chilean export success story in a genre the country rarely gets international recognition for.
