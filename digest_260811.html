Top Headlines
Security

Kast Files 30-Bill Security Package in Senate, Dares Opposition to Move Fast

President José Antonio Kast submitted his sweeping Agenda contra el Crimen Organizado y Terrorismo (ACOT) to the Senate on Monday — a constitutional amendment plus roughly 30 bills aimed at reclaiming Chile's old reputation as the safest country in Latin America. The centerpiece is a new type of state of exception (think: a federally-declared emergency zone) that the president could invoke in neighborhoods dominated by organized crime, starting with the 50 zones currently under heavy Carabineros deployment. The reform would also write public safety explicitly into the constitution as a state duty, and create a formal criminal-organization registry, making "membership" itself a crime — similar to RICO statute logic in the US.

The opposition — a left-center bloc equivalent to a Democratic coalition facing a Republican White House — is divided. The Frente Amplio and Communist Party are resisting, calling the emergency powers authoritarian. Centrist Social Democrats are signaling openness to negotiation. Individual measures poll well above Kast's personal approval (40%): 84% support permanent police presence in high-crime zones, 76% favor letting the military back up police. Kast urged Congress to debate the reform "with high standards and put Chileans first." The legislative math means he needs some opposition votes; the next few weeks in the Senate will be a test of whether either side prefers a deal or a political fight.

Economy

Copper Smashes All-Time Record at $6.56/lb — Chile Celebrates Its Best Week in Years

The price of copper hit a new world record on the London Metal Exchange last Thursday: US$6.557 per pound — up roughly 40% from a year ago and well past any previous high. The rally is driven by structural demand: the energy transition (EVs, wind, solar all require enormous copper wiring), the AI-infrastructure buildout (data centers consume huge amounts), and anticipation of new US tariffs on the metal pushing buyers to stock up early. Economy and Mining Minister Daniel Mas called it "great news" for Chile. Hacienda raised its copper price assumption for budget planning to $5.9/lb, a move that could funnel an additional $1.2–$1.5 billion in tax revenue into the fiscal accounts this year — roughly a windfall the size of a mid-size US city's annual budget, arriving unplanned.

International

Bachelet Trails in Race for UN Secretary General — Grynspan Leads Straw Poll

Chile's former two-term president Michelle Bachelet placed fourth in the Security Council's first informal ballot to choose the next UN Secretary General, receiving 6 votes in favor, 5 against, and 4 abstentions — not enough to emerge as a frontrunner. Rebeca Grynspan of Costa Rica is currently leading the pack. Think of the UN Secretary General role as the world's chief diplomat-for-hire: the 15-member Security Council selects (with the US, Russia, and China each holding veto power) and the full General Assembly approves. Chile formally nominated Bachelet — twice president, a pediatrician by training, and previously executive director of UN Women and the UN Human Rights Council — in a bid to put a Latin American woman at the top of the UN. The selection is expected before year-end, for a 2027–2031 term.

Fiscal Policy

Budget 2027 Build Begins: Max 1% Spending Growth, Copper Boom Could Ease the Math

Chile's Finance Ministry (Hacienda) is assembling the 2027 federal budget — roughly equivalent to the US congressional appropriations process — targeting maximum 1% real spending growth, well below GDP growth, to chip away at the structural deficit. On August 24, expert committees will deliver two critical parameters: the long-term copper price and trend GDP. Those numbers determine how much the government is permitted to spend under Chile's fiscal responsibility rule, similar to a statutory budget cap. The twist: with copper at record highs (see above), the revenue windfall may provide genuine breathing room — potentially allowing modest new spending without technically violating the cap. Hacienda has already cut its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 2.1% but raised its revenue projections on the back of copper.

Astronomy

August Brings Chile Two Free Sky Shows: A Comet Pass and a Lunar Eclipse

BioBioChile's astronomy calendar flags August 2026 as one of the richer months for stargazers in the Southern Hemisphere. Comet P10/Tempel 2 is making its closest approach to Earth this month and is visible with binoculars from dark sites outside Santiago. Then, on the night of August 27–28, Chile and South America have the best-positioned seats on Earth for a partial lunar eclipse — the moon slides into Earth's shadow and turns a deep, coppery red. No ticket, no planning required: just step outside around midnight on the 27th with a clear view of the eastern sky. A good excuse to drive up toward the Cajón del Maipo where light pollution drops sharply.

Opinion
"Constitutional Pyrotechnics" — Kast's Security Reform Is Theater, Not Innovation
Left-leaning · Noticias Los Ríos · Aug 9, 2026

The column argues that Kast's ACOT constitutional reform repackages two things Chile already has: a constitutional duty to guarantee security (Article 1) and emergency-powers language that voters already rejected in the 2022 plebiscite. "What the country witnessed was not a legal innovation, but a layering," the author writes — the same provisions in a new wrapper, dramatized by a prime-time national address. The piece warns that a reform without genuine legal novelty will either die in Congress or, worse, pass as performance while real organized-crime policy stagnates. A left-leaning critique, plainly stated.

FAMFEST 2026 — 19th International Family Theater Festival (Through Aug 23)

Chile's biggest family performing-arts festival runs through August 23 at GAM, Teatro Municipal, Teatro UC, Teatro Mori, and neighborhood venues in La Pintana, Lampa, and San Ramón — even reaching Concepción and Rapa Nui this year. Theme: "Sembremos nuevos mundos" ("Let's plant new worlds"). The program has 33 works spanning theater, dance, circus, opera, and music for every age from infants to teens. Highlights include "GEA" (a dance piece inspired by Chilean biodiversity), "Cachalote" (story of a white whale), "Bloom" (Brazil-Germany co-production mixing poetry, humor, and nature), and "Fauna: una amistad incondicional" — an outdoor free show in GAM's central plaza with aerial dance, acrobatics, giant puppets, and live music. Twenty-one shows include sign-language interpretation. Most tickets are well under $5,000 CLP; several are free.

🎬 Cinema

"Arrau" Documentary at Cineteca Nacional — Sundays Through August

Cristián Vega's documentary about Claudio Arrau — the Chillán-born pianist widely considered one of the greatest of the 20th century, in the same tier as Horowitz and Rubinstein — is screening Sundays at the Cineteca Nacional inside Centro Cultural La Moneda (underground on the Plaza de la Constitución). The film premiered August 6 and runs through the month, with Saturday evening replays on digital TV platforms. Arrau left Chile at age eight for Berlin and never permanently returned, but his story is woven deep into Chilean musical identity. An intimate, quiet afternoon option inside one of the best cinema spaces in the city.

🌙 Outdoors

Partial Lunar Eclipse — Night of Aug 27–28 (Chile Has Best View on Earth)

Mark the calendar: on the night of August 27–28, a partial lunar eclipse turns the moon a deep coppery red as it slides into Earth's shadow, and Chile and South America are positioned better than anywhere else on Earth to watch it. Peak visibility comes in the early hours of the 28th. Drive out toward the Cajón del Maipo or the hillsides east of Las Condes to escape city light pollution — the difference is dramatic. Free, no ticket needed, weather permitting. Comet P10/Tempel 2 is also making a close Earth pass this month and is visible with binoculars from dark sites if you want to make a night of it.

Folk / Folklórico Indie

Cualmarcelo — Calendarios

The debut album from Cualmarcelo, a singer-songwriter from Temuco in Chile's La Araucanía region, landed on digital platforms in April but got its first physical CD release at Matucana 100 on July 30 — a moment that brought the Metropolitana audience face to face with it. The record is described as charged with folk nostalgia: melodic, confessional, deeply rooted in the imagery of the south. Think early Iron & Wine or Bon Iver but rooted in mapuche-influenced southern Chile rather than the US Pacific Northwest. Quiet and unhurried. For fans of: Chinoy, Francisca Valenzuela's acoustic moments, La Moral Disonante.

Metal Dreampop

Diavol Strâin — Eterno Retorno

The most-discussed Chilean alternative record of 2026 so far: Valparaíso/Santiago's Diavol Strâin dropped their fifth album in March and it's still generating attention. Eterno Retorno is maximal darkwave/gothic rock — nine tracks featuring collaborations with William Faith (of LA's Faith & The Muse) and Pablo Giadach (The Cruel Visions). The band works with Bauhaus-style atmospherics and post-punk tension; the international press called it "bracing, full-bodied — possibly the best darkwave album of the year." A limited CD Digipack (150 copies) is out via Bat-Cave Productions if you want the physical. For fans of: Sisters of Mercy, Depeche Mode, early Clan of Xymox.

Red Rockódromo 2026 — 95 Bands, 16 Regions, Running Through October

Chile's national independent music development circuit is midway through its 2026 run: 95 projects were selected from 1,628 applicants across all 16 regions, and the touring showcase kicked off July 10 in Atacama before moving south through the country. It culminates with the XXII National Festival in Valparaíso in October. Think of it as a publicly-funded SXSW showcase spread across the entire country — a Ministry of Cultures pipeline designed to give independent acts their first real regional stages. If you're traveling outside Santiago this month, local venues in almost any region will have a Red Rockódromo stop on the calendar. Rockaxis has a special editorial covering the full circuit.
